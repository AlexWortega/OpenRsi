## H0 — explicit model-separated research loop

- angle: orchestration / independent criticism
- mechanism: independent proposal families → cross-family criticism before implementation → fewer structurally dead experiments reach Sol → more budget is spent on discriminating constructions → higher probability of a verified advance.
- expected delta: at least 50% of proposed ideas are rejected or materially repaired before Sol implementation; zero unreviewed ideas reach implementation.
- falsification: the dry-run does not preserve stage ordering, model identities are not fixed, or Sol can set the final gate.

## H1 — evidence-gated continuation

- angle: verification / budget allocation
- mechanism: machine-readable verifier evidence plus two independent result reviews → false wins and repeated dead routes are stopped → later generations start from a cleaner obstruction map.
- expected delta: every CONTINUE decision contains at least one orchestrator-rerun passing verifier and no failing verifier; unanimous KILL stops the campaign, while a split verdict gets exactly one rebuttal round.
- falsification: CONTINUE is possible from Sol-reported exit codes alone, malformed reviews silently count as approval, or a disagreement loops indefinitely.
