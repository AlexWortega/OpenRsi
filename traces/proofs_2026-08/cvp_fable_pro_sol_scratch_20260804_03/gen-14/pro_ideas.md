1. **Deletion-robust equal-radius Gram design**

**Mechanism.** Optimize a PSD Gram matrix and center so every satisfying one-hot encoding has equal radius, while jointly separating all enumerated signed parities, clause deletions, and short mixed attacks. Unlike G12’s fixed Walsh tag, the entire quadratic geometry is optimized, then rationalized and factored into an explicit lattice.

**Expected move.** Find a symmetry-compatible block family whose separation grows under formula composition.

**Obstruction audit.** G1: no slack. G2/3: does not rely on local affine isolation. G5: Gram terms are global, not private rows. G6: all coordinates enter the emitted CVP objective; unrestricted search is mandatory. G7: zero-residual selectors are charged geometrically. G9/G11: both parity attacks become explicit SDP constraints. G12: clause drops are optimized against simultaneously, rather than post hoc. G13: outside its common-linear-target assumption—honest encodings need equal norm, not equal image.

**Falsification.** SDP optimum fails the desired threshold, or rationalization introduces a short vector.

**Smallest experiment.** Enumerate the existing exact shell through squared radius 108; solve the finite Gram SDP, rationalize, and rerun exact shell DP on obstruction and control.

**Likely death.** Finite separation exists but has no composable asymptotic block rule.

2. **Voltage-lifted matroid with large integer circuits**

**Mechanism.** Replace each factor-graph incidence by a deterministic cyclic or nonabelian voltage lift. Attach selector columns to lifted incidences so a local signed circuit closes only after accumulating large voltage, forcing large support or large coefficients.

**Expected move.** Convert G5’s clause-supported norm-4 circuit into an integer circuit of norm polynomial in the lift order.

**Obstruction audit.** G1: no slack. G2/3: local isolation is replaced by global circuit girth. G5: directly targets overlap-local kernel moves. G6: voltage and normalization rows must all be lattice coordinates. G7: its three-term kernel should fail to close upstairs. G9/G11: parity kernels must either spread through the lift or remain fatal. G12: a clause drop has lifted boundary. G13: not automatically escaped—any implementation sending every honest lift to one syndrome inherits the affine collision; non-common equal-radius sections are required.

**Falsification.** A bounded-support lifted circuit, especially a copied G11 parity, exists for every tested lift.

**Smallest experiment.** Enumerate all cyclic lifts of orders 2–11 of the nine-clause factor graph; compute short integer kernel vectors by SNF plus bounded enumeration.

**Likely death.** Lifted circuits grow only linearly with baseline dimension, yielding no relative gap; or the construction becomes PCP-style repetition.

3. **Algebraic trace-norm fingerprinting**

**Mechanism.** Assign local labels algebraic phases and use the full trace Gram matrix over all conjugates. Honest encodings are arranged to have equal trace norm, while a harmful signed measure should have nonzero algebraic norm, forcing energy across many conjugates.

**Expected move.** Obtain an integer PSD metric where parity, affine-collision, and deletion vectors cannot cancel simultaneously; increase field degree polynomially to amplify energy.

**Obstruction audit.** G1: no slack variables. G2/3: global algebraic geometry replaces local isolation. G5: all occurrences share one field embedding. G6: the trace Gram is emitted exactly and searched unrestrictedly. G7: exact algebraic relations are the immediate test. G9/G11: their signed measures are checked under every conjugate. G12: require every proper clause subsum to retain trace energy. G13: outside common-target hashing because honest points may be distinct conjugate vectors of equal norm.

**Falsification.** A short vanishing sum of phases realizes a known attack, or trace energy scales exactly like completeness energy.

**Smallest experiment.** Search cyclotomic orders up to 64 and label exponents for the existing 72 selectors; construct exact integer trace Grams and enumerate the radius-108 shell.

**Likely death.** Roots of unity have abundant short relations, and algebraic-norm lower bounds may not improve the distance ratio after dimension growth.

4. **Boolean-quotient Macaulay lattice**

**Mechanism.** Work in the squarefree quotient generated by \(x_i^2-x_i\). Stack all degree-\(d\) monomial multiples of the clause-falsity polynomials into a Macaulay matrix \(M_d(F)\), and use the explicit objective  
\[
\|2z-\mathbf1\|^2+\lambda\bigl((z_\varnothing-1)^2+\|M_d(F)z\|^2\bigr).
\]
Satisfying evaluations give normalized Boolean moment vectors in its kernel.

**Expected move.** For unsatisfiable formulas, formula-specific ideal closure eliminates every normalized signed moment vector, permitting large residual scaling.

**Obstruction audit.** G1: no slack. G2/3 and G5: this is one global ideal, not composed local fibers. G6: normalization and all Macaulay rows are internal coordinates. G7: its signed selector must extend to a normalized moment kernel to survive. G9/G11: higher multiples target parity pseudomoments. G12: dropping a clause leaves many monomial-multiple residuals. G13: outside its premise because only satisfying evaluations—not all globally consistent assignments—must share zero residual.

**Falsification.** A normalized rational kernel survives at full degree, or required degree is superconstant.

**Smallest experiment.** For the four-variable obstruction and control, build all 16 squarefree moments, compute exact ranks/SNF, emit the objective, and enumerate short integer \(z\).

**Likely death.** Full Boolean quotient dimension is \(2^n\); bounded-degree closure may retain pseudomoments.

5. **Homogenized tensor-power amplification**

**Mechanism.** Homogenize a fixed-target CVP instance by appending a constant coordinate, then tensor its basis/target \(k\) times. Completeness has a rank-one tensor witness; if arbitrary tensor-lattice combinations preserve a base ratio \(\gamma>1\), the ratio becomes \(\gamma^k\) while dimension becomes \(D^k\).

**Expected move.** Turn a uniform constant-gap base construction into an \(N^{\log_D\gamma}\) gap without constraint repetition.

**Obstruction audit.** This is deliberately not a base repair. G1 slack, G5 overlap circuits, G6 external filtering, and G7 exact kernels all tensor forward if present. G9/G11 parity, G12 deletion, and G13 affine collision likewise remain unless the base lemma excludes them. G2/3 supplies no such global base lemma. Thus the mechanism is outside none of those assumptions; it is only a possible second-stage amplifier.

**Falsification.** Tensor-square distance is below the square of the base distance, caused by sums of mixed or low-rank tensors.

**Smallest experiment.** Tensor-square the exact G9 obstruction/control Gram data, use symmetry-reduced enumeration or branch-and-bound, and compare unrestricted minima with \(96^2\) and \(72^2\).

**Likely death.** Inhomogeneous tensor lattices contain mixed sums much shorter than pure tensor witnesses.

6. **Integral branching-flow encoding**

**Mechanism.** Compile assignment selection and clause checking into a layered automaton; lattice coefficients describe a unit source-to-sink integer flow. A genuine network incidence matrix is totally unimodular, so normalized nonnegative integral flows are paths rather than signed mixtures of local labels.

**Expected move.** Replace selector-affine fibers by one global computation history, making local parity substitutions incompatible with flow conservation.

**Obstruction audit.** G1: no slack. G2/3: TU integrality replaces local isolation. G5: shared variables are read by one history, not glued marginals. G6: source value, transitions, and checks must all be internal. G7/G9/G11: signed substitutions are excluded only if every near-optimal integer flow is nonnegative. G12: deleting a clause state breaks conservation. G13: its negative affine coefficients are not legal nonnegative paths—but CVP itself does not enforce nonnegativity, so this remains an unresolved assumption.

**Falsification.** A negative integral circulation has lower anchor cost than every valid path, or automaton width is exponential.

**Smallest experiment.** Build the minimum-width variable-order automaton for the four-variable obstruction; emit its incidence-based CVP and exhaustively enumerate all short signed flows.

**Likely death.** Euclidean anchors fail to enforce nonnegativity, and polynomial-width automata for arbitrary 3CNF may not exist.

7. **Cohomological torsion/cosystole trap**

**Mechanism.** Realize variables, occurrence agreements, and clause defects as integer cochains on a lifted 2-complex. Arrange that locally invisible signed attacks represent nontrivial cocycles whose minimum representative has large support or torsion order, while honest assignments are coboundary-consistent.

**Expected move.** Turn constant-size parity and overlap kernels into global topological defects with polynomially large Euclidean norm.

**Obstruction audit.** G1: no slack. G2/3: certificates become global cohomology. G5: short overlap circuits are precisely what large cosystole should eliminate. G6: every boundary/coboundary map must appear in the fixed lattice. G7: exact kernels remain possible, but should lie in a large nontrivial cohomology class. G9/G11: test whether cube parity lifts to a small cocycle. G12: a clause drop has nonzero boundary unless it is completed by a large chain. G13: the affine collision still annihilates any common linear syndrome; only a large-support representative theorem could overcome it, so it is not algebraically outside G13.

**Falsification.** A bounded local cocycle or boundary filling survives every small lift.

**Smallest experiment.** Construct 2-complexes from cyclic lifts of the nine-clause incidence graph; compute integer homology, torsion, and minimum-support representatives.

**Likely death.** Formula-derived complexes may have poor cosystole, and obtaining robust expansion may secretly reintroduce PCP machinery.
