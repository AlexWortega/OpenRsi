No mechanism below is promoted; each begins with an executable obstruction test.

### 1. Signed-robust mixed-radix knapsack

**Core trick / expected move.** Start from Karp’s direct SAT-to-subset-sum digits, but choose several coprime mixed-radix orderings so every exact *integer* representation of the target is either Boolean or has coefficient norm \(n^{\Omega(1)}\). Scale the single knapsack residual above the completeness radius.

**Obstruction audit.** G1: slack coefficients are anchored items, not free residual cancellers, though negative slack remains dangerous. G2/3 and G5: bypass local isolation/private-row composition with one global digit system. G6: emit every digit and anchor coordinate. G7: unlike residual radix, radix is intended to make the underlying item representation injective; an exact signed relation still kills it. G9/11: no bounded moments. G12: a drop leaves a scaled target deficit. G13/G15: not a compatible hash of raw selectors, but an affine subset-sum representation may still exist. G14: no fixed pair-bag claim. G19: no flow. G20/21/G22: polynomial excess-to-baseline scaling is wholly unproved.

**Falsification / experiment.** Build the all-eight-clause instance with three radix orders; use SNF plus enumeration to find the minimum-norm exact integer solution, killing on excess \(\le16\).

**Likely death.** A short Bézout/carry relation survives every ordering.

### 2. Zero-centered logarithmic bags

**Core trick / expected move.** Replace the pair-bag anchor \(\|2z-1\|^2\), whose baseline counts every possible label, by \(\|z\|^2\) plus heavily weighted normalization: an honest one-hot bag costs \(1\), while signed support is charged directly. Use perfect-hash families of \(k=\Theta(\log n)\)-variable bags, hoping every zero-residual unsatisfiable marginal family has polynomially growing integer coefficient energy.

**Obstruction audit.** G1: no slack. G2/3/G5: growing complete bags replace local fixed-marginal isolation. G6: normalization and consistency are emitted. G7: no radix. G9/11: bags contain complete assignments, not degree-\(\le3\) moments. G12: dropping a bag incurs the scaled normalization row. G13 and G15 remain fully applicable: their affine lift may have zero residual; only its norm is newly charged. G14: this specifically changes its baseline geometry and bag width. G19: no flow, although signed marginal splicing is analogous. G20/21/G22: the required asymptotic norm lower bound is missing.

**Falsification / experiment.** Modify `verify_pair_bag_lift.py` to zero-centered anchors; test all bags on \(3\) and \(4\) variables and exactly compare the known G13 lift with the control.

**Likely death.** The thirteen-term affine certificate has constant energy per bag, giving only a constant ratio.

### 3. Full Boolean-quotient moment functional

**Core trick / expected move.** Work in the Boolean coordinate ring generated by \(x_i^2-x_i\). Introduce a linear functional \(L\) on all squarefree monomials, impose \(L(1)=1\) and \(L(mf_C)=0\) for every clause polynomial \(f_C\) and every complementary monomial \(m\); satisfiability gives an integral evaluation functional, while an unsatisfiable formula makes \(1\) belong to the clause ideal.

**Obstruction audit.** G1: no slack. G2/3/G5: this is one global quotient, not overlapping local fibers. G6: every Macaulay equation is a CVP coordinate. G7: no radix. G9/11: full degree removes the unique-triple loophole. G12: deleting normalization is heavily charged. G13/G15: at full degree, a normalized affine pseudodistribution annihilating the ideal would be a signed measure supported on common Boolean zeros; none exist when unsatisfiable. G14: no bounded bags. G19: no flow. G20/21/G22: the full \(2^n\)-dimensional quotient violates polynomial size; compression is exactly the unresolved gate.

**Falsification / experiment.** For the nine-clause four-variable obstruction, emit all \(16\) monomials and the complete multiplication closure; verify exact inconsistency over \(\mathbb Q\), then compute unrestricted CVP minima against a satisfiable control.

**Likely death.** Any sound compression recreates low-degree pseudodistributions or requires exponential dimension.

### 4. Torsion mapping-telescope filling

**Core trick / expected move.** Build a clause-variable cell complex whose legal local labels are oriented 2-cells and whose normalization target is a fixed 1-cycle \(\gamma\). Attach a degree-\(D\) mapping telescope so that, if unsatisfiability leaves \([\gamma]\) as nonzero torsion, every integral filling has coefficients \(\Omega(D)\), while a satisfying assignment supplies a small direct filling.

**Obstruction audit.** G1: no slack. G2/3/G5: obstruction is a global homology class rather than fixed local marginals. G6: boundary maps and \(\gamma\) must be emitted explicitly. G7: no radix. G9/11: no moment truncation. G12: a missing cell creates boundary residual. G13/G15: outside their assumptions only if the affine pseudosection has nonzero torsion class; if it is null-homologous, they kill the idea unchanged. G14: no pair-bag shell claim. G19: signed chains are the direct analogue of signed flows. G20/21: telescope depth must yield relative, not merely absolute, growth. G22’s homology blocker remains until the class survives SNF.

**Falsification / experiment.** Construct the canonical incidence complex for the all-eight-clause formula, compute SNF and the class of \(\gamma\) before adding any telescope; kill immediately if \([\gamma]=0\).

**Likely death.** The known affine combination is already a short integral filling.

### 5. Totally-positive hyperbolic transport

**Core trick / expected move.** Replace permutation-state transport in the branching program by noncommuting totally-positive matrices such as Stern–Brocot generators and several symmetric-power representations. Distinct words then separate projectively; the hope is that any signed splice with the correct source and accepting boundary needs exponentially large cancellation coefficients.

**Obstruction audit.** G1: no slack. G2/3/G5: transport is global across all layers. G6: all transported state coordinates must be emitted. G7: no radix. G9/11: these are noncommutative dynamical states, not commutative moments, although symmetric powers are still linear lifts. G12: dropping a layer violates scaled conservation. G13/G15 remain dangerous because affine combinations transport linearly. G14: no bags. G19 applies directly: the construction must quantitatively enlarge its two-negative-edge splice rather than merely retag it. G20/21: coefficient growth must beat the transported-state baseline. G22’s linear-representation objection is not escaped until an explicit anti-cancellation bound is demonstrated.

**Falsification / experiment.** Compile the toy contradiction \(x\land\neg x\), append symmetric powers \(1,2,3\) of two fixed \(SL_2(\mathbb Z)\) generators, and exactly search zero-residual states through anchor excess \(16\).

**Likely death.** Low-dimensional determinant identities produce another constant-support signed splice.

### 6. Recursive Delaunay empty-sphere gadget

**Core trick / expected move.** Search directly for a rational lattice metric and center whose nearest lattice points are exactly the legal local encodings, with all known signed selectors and drops beyond a certified second shell. Attempt a laminated or tensorized Delaunay construction in which the second-shell/completeness ratio grows multiplicatively rather than merely adding energy.

**Obstruction audit.** G1: legality is geometric, without slack. G2/3/G5: one empty-sphere certificate replaces private affine fibers. G6: emit \(Q,t\), a rational factorization, and an exact shell certificate. G7: no radix. G9/11: this remains inside the broad global-PSD paradigm, but does not restrict \(Q\) to moment residuals; those finite results therefore warn rather than formally kill it. G12: drops are included among forbidden inner-shell lattice points. G13/G15: their affine combinations remain lattice points and must be geometrically far—no automatic escape. G14: no fixed pair-bag theorem. G19: no flow. G20/21: multiplicative shell scaling is the essential missing lemma. G22’s tensor-composition and entangled-short-vector objections apply directly.

**Falsification / experiment.** Use SDP followed by rational reconstruction on the 72-selector obstruction, constraining equal honest radii and explicitly separating every archived G7/G11/G12/G13 attack; then enumerate the complete certified shell.

**Likely death.** Tensor/lamination introduces entangled short vectors, and relative shell gaps collapse toward \(1\).
