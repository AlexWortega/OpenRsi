All smallest experiments below use the existing nine-clause obstruction and matched control unless stated otherwise.

1. **Positive-definite Gram repair of the exterior tags.**  
**Mechanism.** Retain Generation-33’s eight bivectors, but search jointly for a rational metric \(G\succeq I/100\), \(\operatorname{tr}G=1\), and formula-specific centers making all 16 honest fingerprints cospherical. A learned metric could charge parity directions that the Euclidean metric misses without collapsing tag variation.  
**Expected move.** Pass completeness, then exceed the G31 \(4/3\) shell against parity and DROP.  
**Map check.** No RS slack (G1), affine isolation/audit (G2–3), private overlap (G5), radix (G7), bags/hierarchy (G14–15), flow (G19), tiles (G28), or tensoring (G30); every row is emitted, satisfying G6. It varies the fixed PSD/moment/tag metrics of G9/G11/G12. Quadratic equal-radius separation is outside G13’s common-linear-syndrome assumption. G31 is the same broad geometric class, so not escaped; G32 composition remains open. It is outside G33’s fixed \(G=I\), while directly respecting that failure.  
**Falsifier.** Exact infeasibility, singular-only solutions, no exact factor, or parity/DROP below threshold.  
**Experiment.** Enumerate all 512 sign rules; solve the prescribed rational SDP feasibility system, rationally reconstruct \(G\), and emit LDL/dual certificates before any shell search.

2. **Nonabelian Fourier fingerprints.**  
**Mechanism.** Assign variable-dependent generators of a small nonabelian group and tag each clause label by all matrix entries of several irreducible representations of its ordered generator word. Sum these in shared representation blocks; noncommuting cross terms in squared norm can detect cube parity even when all proper commutative moments agree.  
**Expected move.** Find a common sphere whose parity and two-copy parity energies are strictly superadditive.  
**Map check.** No slack/local hash/private overlap/external filtering/radix (G1–7). Unlike G9/G11 it is not a degree-\(\le3\) commutative moment metric; unlike G12 it is not one scalar clause tag. Honest fingerprints are cospherical, not equal, so G13’s common-syndrome theorem does not apply. It uses neither G14 bags, G15 hierarchy, G19 flow, G28 tiles, nor G30 tensoring. It lies near G31’s equal-radius class but outside its block-diagonal \(H_8\); G32 cancellation is not automatically excluded. It is not G33’s bivector family.  
**Falsifier.** Completeness is non-cospherical, or compatible parity blocks cancel in every representation.  
**Experiment.** Try \(S_3,D_8,Q_8\), representations of dimension at most two, and all generator assignments; certify spheres exactly and enumerate G7/G11/DROP/two-copy attacks.

3. **Non-unimodular torsion sheaf.**  
**Mechanism.** Replace identity marginal glue by label-preserving integer maps of determinant \(q>1\), arranged along a depth-\(L\) occurrence network. A harmful local section should require divisibility by \(q\) at each extension, forcing coefficients of size \(q^L\), while designated one-hot sections remain primitive.  
**Expected move.** With \(L=\Theta(\log n)\), obtain polynomial coefficient—and hence Euclidean-distance—growth from exact zero-residual cheats.  
**Map check.** Its anchored digits are outside G1’s free slack, though a carry analogue must be audited. G2–3 concern fixed local isolation; G5 identity/private overlap; G6 is met by emitting all maps. G7’s zero kernel is addressed by making kernel sections large, not by radix. G9/G11/G12/G31/G33 are metric/tag constructions, unlike this arithmetic index mechanism. G14/G28 used identity glue; G19/G30 are flow/tensor routes. Crucially, G13 affine collision and G15 affine lifting are **not** automatically escaped; they may produce a primitive section. G32 additivity also remains possible.  
**Falsifier.** SNF reveals a primitive harmful section, or honest labels cannot all remain integral.  
**Experiment.** Search \(2\times2\) maps with determinant \(2\) on chains of depth 1–6; compute exact harmful-fiber SNFs and shortest vectors.

4. **Cosystolic lift of selector deviations.**  
**Mechanism.** Place clause deviations as integer \(1\)-cochains on a bounded-degree \(2\)-complex, with overlap checks as coboundaries and additional face selectors killing locally supported cycles. Cosystolic expansion could force every exact harmful cocycle to occupy many cells; iterated dimensions might turn local parity into global support growth.  
**Expected move.** Establish an integer coisoperimetric inequality linking anchor excess to complex size, then test whether weighted iteration yields polynomial—not merely constant—distance ratio.  
**Map check.** No slack, local hash, private-row composition, filtering, or radix (G1–7). It is topological rather than the fixed moments/tags of G9/G11/G12/G31/G33. G13 affine parity and G15’s hierarchy lift are not escaped unless their cochains have nonzero higher coboundary. G14 is only a complete pair mesh, not a higher-dimensional complex. G19 flow is one-dimensional; G28/G30 use min-plus/tensor composition. G32 additive parity may survive as disjoint cocycles, so superadditivity is unproved. G6 requires every coboundary row to be emitted.  
**Falsifier.** A bounded-support integral cocycle, torsion class, or only linear extra cost comparable to completeness.  
**Experiment.** Build the tetrahedral, octahedral, and smallest available bounded-degree complexes; attach the G31 local state table and enumerate integral cochains through anchor excess 48.

5. **Compound-matrix rather than literal-tensor amplification.**  
**Mechanism.** From an integer factor \(C\), construct its \(k\)-th compound matrix \(C^{(k)}\), whose entries are minors, and similarly transform the target after homogenization. Binet–Cauchy suggests multiplicative separation for decomposable witnesses, while antisymmetry removes diagonal tensor shortcuts.  
**Expected move.** Find a non-isometric seed for which the unrestricted second-compound NO/YES ratio exceeds the base ratio.  
**Map check.** No slack/hashes/overlap/filter/radix (G1–7), moments/tags (G9/G11/G12/G31), bags/hierarchy/flow/tiles (G14/G15/G19/G28), or raw-selector syndrome (G13). It is outside G30’s literal Kronecker product and its seed isometry. G32’s additive parity has no canonical compound lift, though unrestricted compound coefficients may recreate it. It differs from G33: exterior algebra acts on the whole factor, not fixed local bivector tags, so G33’s cosphere failure does not apply. All compound rows and target coordinates are emitted, satisfying G6.  
**Falsifier.** A cheap nondecomposable coefficient vector, completeness mismatch, or ratio nonincrease.  
**Experiment.** Choose the smallest non-isometric rank-6 G28-derived seed, emit \(C^{(2)}\), and exactly enumerate all compound coefficients in the radius implied by the base witness.

6. **Ideal-lattice norm divisibility.**  
**Mechanism.** Encode selector consistency as a coset of an ideal module in a low-degree number field. Arrange clause failure to force divisibility of the algebraic norm by a product of explicit split primes; under the Minkowski embedding, AM–GM then converts that product into a Euclidean lower bound.  
**Expected move.** Accumulate \(\Theta(\log n)\) prime factors while keeping field degree and lattice dimension polynomial, producing \(n^c\) distance.  
**Map check.** This has no free slack (G1), local affine hash (G2–3), private overlap (G5), radix row (G7), moment/tag metric (G9/G11/G12/G31/G33), bag hierarchy (G14–15), flow (G19), tile recursion (G28), or tensor seed (G30); the entire ideal basis/coset is emitted, meeting G6. G13 is **not** escaped if its affine collision remains valid over the ring, and G32 may simply add independent ideal witnesses. The nonlinear norm consequence—not a compatible linear syndrome—is the only proposed distinction.  
**Falsifier.** A unit or affine selector gives small norm, or clause unsatisfaction cannot force prime divisibility without multiplication constraints.  
**Experiment.** In \(\mathbb Z[\sqrt5]\), use two split primes, emit the module for one falsified OR core, and enumerate Minkowski vectors while checking exact ideal valuations.

7. **Logarithmic splitter bags with nonlinear list-recovery tags.**  
**Mechanism.** Use a deterministic family of \(k=\Theta(\log n)\)-variable bags such that every small signed support is isolated in some bag. Give each full bag assignment a nonlinear constant-weight codeword and enforce complete overlap marginals; dimensions remain polynomial because \(2^k=n^{O(1)}\).  
**Expected move.** Turn every sparse signed-selector attack into large code distance in at least one bag, then determine whether dense attacks admit a separate averaging bound.  
**Map check.** No slack/local hash/private rows/filter/radix (G1–7). Full assignments, not degree-2/3 moments, avoid G9/G11; nonlinear bag codewords differ from G12. They are outside G13’s **raw-selector** syndrome, but its affine global combination can still thread all marginal rows and must be charged geometrically. G14 uses only pair bags; G15 is laminar rather than overlapping splitters. No flow/tile/tensor (G19/G28/G30). This extends G31’s equal-radius idea, so its general limitations remain; G32 compatible parity and G33 cosphericity are mandatory tests.  
**Falsifier.** A dense affine pseudodistribution, non-cospherical code, or baseline growth swamps the code penalty.  
**Experiment.** On four variables, use all 3-variable bags; exhaust constant-weight binary tags of length at most 12, certify completeness, and run the unrestricted shell through G31’s \(4/3\) threshold.
