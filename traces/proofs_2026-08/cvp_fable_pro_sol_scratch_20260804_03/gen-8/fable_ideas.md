I use these obstruction labels throughout:

- **O1 (G1):** free integer slack annihilates amplified residuals.
- **O2 (G2–3):** bounded local isolation is insufficient; affine fibers require exact unbounded certification.
- **O3 (G5):** private local isolation collapses when overlapping clauses free unshared marginals.
- **O4 (G6):** external filters, changed references, or missed modular bypasses are not valid CVP evidence.
- **O5 (G7):** the exact signed identity `011 + 100 − 111 = 000` defeats every linear weighting of the old residuals.
- **O6:** no polynomial family, gap law, or reduction theorem yet exists.

## 1. Higher-moment bag lift

**Core trick.** Replace first marginals by the full multilinear feature vector  
\(\phi(p)=(1,p_i,p_ip_j,p_1p_2p_3)\). Degree two already detects O5 because, for example, the \(p_1p_2\) coordinate of `011 + 100 − 111` is \(-1\), not the coordinate of `000`; global consistency is imposed on moments of overlapping variable bags.

**Expected move.** Find a small level \(k\) at which every normalized integral pseudoassignment to the nine-clause obstruction has nonzero syndrome, then code-spread that syndrome.

**Obstruction audit.** O1: no slack. O2: certify fibers by exact rational elimination/SNF. O3: not escaped—higher-moment overlap is precisely the new composition test. O4: emit every moment and consistency row in one fixed basis. O5: outside the old first-marginal assumptions. O6: still open; constant \(k\) is essential.

**Falsification.** Any normalized zero-syndrome integral pseudomoment on the obstruction kills that \(k\).

**Smallest experiment.** Implement \(k=2,3\), exact-CVP/SNF audit on the nine-clause instance and a polarity-matched satisfiable control.

**Likely death.** Signed pseudoexpectations survive until \(k=\Omega(n)\), making dimension exponential.

## 2. Trellis range-lock for clause slack

**Core trick.** Return to \(L_1+L_2+L_3=1+s\), but represent legal \(s\in\{0,1,2\}\) by an accepting path in a layered finite-state trellis rather than a free integer coefficient. Add coded transition tags so an integral flow purporting to encode \(s=-1\) must either violate a row or use many non-Boolean transition coefficients.

**Expected move.** Convert the G1 slack cheat from constant anchor excess into \(\Omega(L)\), with \(L\) polynomially selectable.

**Obstruction audit.** O1: directly targeted; no free slack column exists. O2: analyze all integral trellis flows exactly, not in a coefficient box. O3: clauses share global literal variables, not private selector marginals. O4: start/end states, transitions, tags, and target are all lattice coordinates. O5: the old selector identity is absent, although signed trellis circulations are a new affine-kernel risk. O6: amplification relative to honest trellis cost remains unproved.

**Falsification.** A flow encoding \(-1\) with \(O(1)\) excess cost independent of \(L\).

**Smallest experiment.** For \(L=4,\ldots,8\), build the all-eight-clauses instance, enumerate short integral circulations, and solve unrestricted exact CVP.

**Likely death.** Negative flow cycles defeat range locking, or honest path cost grows at the same rate as cheating cost.

## 3. Boolean Nullstellensatz/Macaulay obstruction

**Core trick.** Encode clauses as Boolean polynomials and construct the degree-\(d\) Macaulay system for the ideal generated by clause polynomials and \(x_i^2-x_i\). A satisfying assignment gives an evaluation moment in the kernel, while an unsatisfiable system may admit an exact certificate \(1=\sum g_if_i+\sum h_j(x_j^2-x_j)\); effective bounds are classical, e.g. Kollár (1988).

**Expected move.** Turn the certificate into an integral left-kernel separator, then apply an explicit code to any nonzero Macaulay residual.

**Obstruction audit.** O1: no clause slack. O2: rational elimination and SNF give unbounded certificates. O3: the ideal is global, so overlap is not handled by private rows. O4: normalization \(y_{\emptyset}=1\) and every multiplication row must be embedded in the fixed CVP target. O5: selector variables and their identity disappear. O6: degree and coefficient growth are the unresolved bottlenecks.

**Falsification.** A normalized rational Macaulay kernel vector on the unsatisfiable instance at tested degree.

**Smallest experiment.** Construct square-free degree \(d=1,\ldots,4\) matrices for the nine-clause formula; compute kernels, SNF, and exact CVP, with a `0000`-satisfiable polarity control.

**Likely death.** Required degree is linear, hence the monomial lift is exponential.

## 4. Homological syndrome with cosystolic expansion

**Core trick.** Map clause defects and occurrence disagreements to cochains in an explicit bounded-degree chain complex, arranging the target as a nonzero syndrome coset exactly when the formula is unsatisfied. Add higher-dimensional incidence checks so the old local signed selector circuit carries a nonzero cochain, and seek a cosystolic lower bound forcing every representative to have large support (classical antecedents: Tseitin 1968; Sipser–Spielman 1996).

**Expected move.** Replace “one violated clause” by a topologically protected defect of polynomial Euclidean norm.

**Obstruction audit.** O1: \(q\)-multiple lattice generators cannot erase a genuinely nonzero mod-\(q\) coset. O2: homology and shortest coset representatives are audited exactly. O3: the complex is global, not private-row composition. O4: all boundary maps and modular generators are emitted; no filtered chains. O5: outside only if the added incidence map actually sends the signed identity nontrivially—this must be tested. O6: a uniform expansion theorem and reduction are absent.

**Falsification.** The O5 attack has zero syndrome, or the target coset has support below \(L/4\).

**Smallest experiment.** Attach candidate 2-cells to the nine-clause incidence complex and enumerate all short cochains over \(\mathbb F_2,\mathbb F_3\).

**Likely death.** Constructing the needed expansion from arbitrary 3SAT secretly requires PCP-style gap amplification.

## 5. Dissociated nonlinear label commitments

**Core trick.** Give every local pattern a nonlinear tag from an explicit \(B_h\)/dissociated set, and commit shared assignments to corresponding hash coordinates. Choose tags so no signed relation of \(\ell_1\)-weight at most \(H\) can replace a forbidden label while preserving normalization, shared hashes, and clause legality (compare Bose–Chowla, 1962).

**Expected move.** Force every affine-kernel cheat to use at least \(H=n^\alpha\) non-Boolean coefficients, making its anchor excess polynomially large before residual coding.

**Obstruction audit.** O1: no slack. O2: dissociation is only bounded, but a candidate CVP radius must itself imply the \(\ell_1\le H\) bound; otherwise O2 applies. O3: tags are shared assignment commitments, not private rows, though composition remains unproved. O4: hash commitments and all coupling equations are internal coordinates. O5: the dictionary is explicitly required to reject that relation. O6: polynomial-size tags and a favorable dimension/radius calculation are missing.

**Falsification.** Any shared-hash-preserving signed relation of weight at most \(H\).

**Smallest experiment.** Search integer tag vectors in dimensions 2–5 for \(H=6,8\); compose them on every one/two-variable overlap and then solve the nine-clause fixed-target CVP.

**Likely death.** Nonlinear tags cannot be coupled consistently to global bits using only linear lattice equations.

## 6. Tensor-rank amplification after kernel removal

**Core trick.** Once a seed syndrome map has no exact harmful kernel, lift selectors to pair or \(k\)-tuple variables and encode tensor powers of the syndrome. A nonzero seed defect should then occupy many tensor coordinates, potentially multiplying the no-case distance while \(k\) remains constant and dimension polynomial.

**Expected move.** Obtain a genuine gap-composition lemma rather than merely increasing residual weights.

**Obstruction audit.** O1: use a slack-free seed. O2: pair-variable fibers require exact rational/SNF audit. O3: tensor variables must be global across overlaps, not clause-private. O4: rank-consistency equations and targets must all be emitted—no post hoc “rank-one” filter. O5: not escaped by tensoring the old matrix, since \(0^{\otimes k}=0\); a kernel-free seed such as a moment lift is mandatory. O6: this is aimed at O6, but no multiplicative distance theorem exists.

**Falsification.** A non-rank-one integral pseudo-tensor with zero amplified syndrome and small anchor excess.

**Smallest experiment.** Build the \(k=2\) lift of the degree-two moment system on the nine-clause instance; enumerate low-weight pair variables and run exact CVP against a satisfiable control.

**Likely death.** Linear rank-consistency constraints admit transportation-polytope pseudo-tensors, and enforcing decomposability causes an infinite lifting hierarchy.

## 7. Composable gadget synthesis via integer polymorphisms

**Core trick.** Automatically synthesize a constant-size Boolean auxiliary gadget whose legal configurations have no short integer-affine operation projecting to the forbidden clause tuple. Unlike G2, candidates are accepted only after variable identification on every boundary overlap; this treats the gadget as a pp-definition/composable relation rather than a private measurement matrix.

**Expected move.** Discover a constant-size “affine-polymorphism breaker” that can replace every clause before applying residual coding.

**Obstruction audit.** O1: search gadgets without free slack. O2: use exact left-kernel certificates and Graver/SNF computation, never boxed enumeration alone. O3: two- and three-gadget overlap is part of acceptance, so the killed private-row route is not repeated unchanged. O4: auxiliary normalization and consistency rows are included in the emitted basis and fixed target. O5: reject any gadget admitting the precise `011 + 100 − 111` projection or another short replacement. O6: even a survivor supplies only a kernel-removal primitive, not yet a polynomial gap.

**Falsification.** Every gadget below the size cap has a short projected affine circuit after overlap.

**Smallest experiment.** Enumerate gadgets with at most two auxiliary bits and four 3-CNF relations; audit all overlap types exactly, then instantiate survivors on the nine-clause formula.

**Likely death.** Integer-affine circuits are preserved under all small Boolean gadget extensions, or gadget baseline cost overwhelms later amplification.
