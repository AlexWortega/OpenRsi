## 1. Protected-OR composition of asymmetric folds

**Core trick.** For polynomially many deterministic folds \((H_i,t_i)\), introduce selectors \(s_i\) and disjoint equations \(H_i x_i=s_i t_i\), \(\sum_i s_i=1\pmod2\). The resulting coset distance is exactly \(\min_{i:s_i=1}(|x_i|+1)\): odd superpositions of branches add disjoint costs rather than splice them.

**Expected move.** A polynomial fold family need only cover every YES witness with one cheap branch, while every branch remains expensive on NO instances.

**Obstruction check.** O1 **bounded signatures**, O2 **marginals/tableaus**, O3 **local scopes**: branches are whole global affine fibers. O4 **phase lifts**: this is not a phase; disjointness proves selector soundness. O5 **integer fibers**: binary construction. O6 **assignment fingerprints**: no assignment columns. O7 **tensor amplification**: every mixed word inside every branch still requires verification. O8 **exact transfer** applies directly.

**Smallest experiment/falsification.** On current \(q=3,r=2\) tensors, enumerate canonical dense \(8\)-output folds, solve a set-cover problem over all YES fibers subject to NO distance \(\ge B\), compile the best 2–4 branches, and enumerate all words on all-eight and twisted-holonomy instances. Kill unless total rank gives a better exponent than the unfurled tensor.

**Likely death.** Any adequate covering family is exponential or implicitly identifies witnesses.

## 2. Multiplicity-PIT folding of mixed tensor polynomials

**Core trick.** Represent a reduced \(r\)-tensor word as a polynomial \(P(y_1,\ldots,y_r)\) in message variables. Restrict it to a deterministic family of Kronecker curves over \(\mathbb F_{2^s}\), retaining values and Hasse derivatives; encode each field symbol by a simplex block, so every nonzero symbol has equal binary weight.

**Expected move.** Multiplicity identity testing might prevent arbitrary mixed words from vanishing while using polynomially many evaluations instead of tensor coordinates.

**Obstruction check.** O1 **bounded signatures**: degree grows with \(r\) and the map is global, though embedded cubes remain dangerous. O2 **tableaus**, O3 **local scopes**, O4 **phases**, O5 **integer fibers** do not apply. O6 **fingerprints**: variables are code messages, not complete assignments. O7 **tensor amplification** is the central obligation—pure tensors are insufficient. O8 **transfer** applies after the linear simplex embedding and parity-check extraction.

**Smallest experiment/falsification.** For \(q=3,r=2\), freeze \(\mathbb F_{16}\), two canonical exponent curves, and derivative order one. Enumerate every mixed word for ten YES/NO codes plus all-eight and holonomy cases; record binary rank, worst YES, and best NO. Kill on any pointed kernel word or exponent below the unfurled square.

**Likely death.** High-rank mixed polynomials vanish on every polynomial-size curve family; derivatives merely increase baseline.

## 3. Nonsemisimple algebra multiplication fold

**Core trick.** Map coordinate \(j\) to a canonical element \(a_j\) of \(A=\mathbb F_2[u]/(u^M)\), then fold \(e_{i_1}\otimes\cdots\otimes e_{i_r}\) to the coefficient vector of \(a_{i_1}\cdots a_{i_r}\). Multiplication is a dense linear map on tensor coordinates, has output length \(M\), and can be iterated without forming \(M^r\) coordinates.

**Expected move.** A nilpotent filtration could make legal representatives occupy a few valuation layers while forcing NO mixed words across many layers. This differs from cyclic closure and pure catalysts: the instance code is unchanged and the fold is asymmetric, nonsemisimple multiplication.

**Obstruction check.** O1 **bounded signatures**: multiplication has growing global degree; all-eight cancellation remains unexcluded. O2 **tableaus**, O3 **scopes**, O4 **phases**, O5 **integer fibers**, O6 **assignment fingerprints** are absent. O7 **tensor amplification** is addressed only if every mixed product combination has distance. O8 **transfer** applies because multiplication is binary linear.

**Smallest experiment/falsification.** Set \(M=16\), \(a_j=1+u^{h_j}+u^{2h_j+1}\), with \(h_j\) derived canonically from parity-check columns. Fold current reduced squares and exhaust all mixed words, all-eight, and holonomy cases. Kill if NO distance reaches zero, or if rank-adjusted gap fails to improve.

**Likely death.** Zero divisors and leading-term cancellation create tiny NO words.

## 4. Global quadratic metric on the signed exact-cover fiber

**Core trick.** Replace the ordinary coefficient norm in integer 3DM CVP by \(z^{T}G(A)z\), where \(G(A)\succeq0\) lies in the polynomial-dimensional algebra generated by triple-overlap matrices. Choose its coefficients deterministically from spectral data of \(A\), then rationally factor and scale \(G=R^{T}R\) in the explicit basis \([R;MA]\).

**Expected move.** Global correlations might give every perfect matching cost \(O(q)\) while spreading each signed nonmatching exact cover over \(q^{1+\epsilon}\) energy.

**Obstruction check.** O1 **bounded signatures** is formally outside because the objective is a global quadratic form, but cube trades may still imply constant-factor upper bounds. O2 **tableaus**, O3 **scopes**, O4 **phases**, O6 **fingerprints**, O7 **tensoring** are unused. O5 **integer exact fibers** is the principal threat: it does not explicitly cover dense quadratic metrics, but few-coordinate repairs may remain cheap. O8 **exact transfer** does not apply; this currently bypasses the required binary route.

**Smallest experiment/falsification.** For all \(q=3\) dictionaries, optimize invariant \(G\) by SDP, rationalize it, and enumerate every \(z\in[-2,2]^m\) with \(Az=1\), including all-eight and holonomy examples. Require transfer across relabelings and improved rank exponent.

**Likely death.** PSD constraints make amplifying one defect amplify the YES baseline; binary compilation may be impossible.

## 5. Higher-Lawrence/Graver amplification

**Core trick.** Apply a higher Lawrence lift to the homogenized incidence matrix, coupling many exact-cover bricks through one global sum row. Legal matchings should admit a sparse conformal decomposition, whereas a signed NO repair may become a high-complexity Graver element whose \(\ell_1\) or parity support grows with the number of bricks.

**Expected move.** Obtain multiplicative defect growth from integer-programming circuit complexity with only linear or quadratic matrix growth, rather than tensor rank.

**Obstruction check.** O1 **bounded signatures**, O2 **tableaus**, O3 **scopes**, O4 **phases**, O6 **fingerprints** are absent: the lift acts on the global incidence matrix. O5 **integer exact fibers** directly threatens this route because the lifted system remains affine; survival requires proving that constant repairs cannot be placed in one or two bricks. O7 **tensor amplification** is avoided, though all mixed/conformal brick combinations replace that obligation. O8 **transfer** applies only if the same separation survives reduction modulo two.

**Smallest experiment/falsification.** Build second and third Lawrence lifts of every \(q=3\) YES/NO matrix. Use exact ILP/Graver enumeration for pointed minima, then repeat modulo two; include all-eight and twisted-holonomy matrices. Kill if a diagonal pair or two-brick repair preserves the base ratio, or output-rank exponent decreases.

**Likely death.** A NO witness and its negative fit in two bricks, defeating Graver growth.

## 6. Multiplicity-code base reduction rather than post hoc tensoring

**Core trick.** Start from a global Reed–Solomon-style sparse-interpolation hardness instance, then replace ordinary evaluations by multiplicity evaluations on an explicit algebraic-curve tower. A satisfying interpolation should alter few symbol blocks, while an invalid interpolation defect of multiplicity \(r\) should lose agreement at many derivative coordinates without explicit Cartesian powering.

**Expected move.** Produce the polynomial gap in the base code itself, with polynomial evaluation length and no subsequent tensor compression.

**Obstruction check.** O1 **bounded signatures**: the witness is one global high-degree polynomial, not a bounded Boolean signature. O2 **marginals/tableaus**, O3 **local scopes**, O4 **phases**, O5 **integer fibers** do not apply. O6 **assignment fingerprints**: columns are error locations, not complete assignments, although the interpolation reduction could secretly enumerate witnesses. O7 **tensor amplification** is avoided; soundness must instead cover every polynomial in the affine decoding fiber. O8 **transfer** applies after an \(\mathbb F_2\)-linear simplex encoding of symbols.

**Smallest experiment/falsification.** Reproduce the smallest exact interpolation instance, add first and second Hasse derivatives, enumerate all candidate polynomials, and convert the affine image to a binary parity-check matrix. Include algebraic analogues of all-eight and odd-holonomy unions; compare worst YES, best NO, and binary rank.

**Likely death.** Multiplicity increases distance only additively, while simplex encoding and derivatives inflate the YES baseline.
