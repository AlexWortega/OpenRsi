1. **Redundant residue-code gate**

**Core trick.** Replace one-selector-per-truth-word by four binary codewords in \(\{0,1\}^N\), chosen so every integral affine representation of a false NAND boundary has large anchor norm. Coordinate-signature multiplicities turn the \(N\le16\) search into coding-theoretic optimization over only 16 signature types.

**Expected move.** Produce a module with every false fiber costing at least the explicitly derived \(\mathfrak P\)-minimum \(34\), and ratio \(>17/16\), thereby supplying a viable local seed for the FRONTIER.

**Obstruction audit.** G1 RS slack, G6 external quotient, G7 radix kernel, and G12/DROP are emitted fibers, not filters. G2 affine/Graver and G3 unbounded fibers are handled exactly by SNF plus unbounded optimization; G14 pair bags, G31 Walsh, and G38 splitter conclusions do not scale this seed. G5 private overlap, G9/G11 parity, G13 affine collision, and G15 laminar lift are explicit false fibers. G19 signed flow, GD1 diagonal closure, and ordered-pair splice remain unescaped until depth two. G28 min-plus, G32 additive parity, G37 parity cut, and G30 tensor are not used. GD2/A5 convolution, G33/G34 exterior metrics, all D4 shell obstructions, and E6 affine-port no-gos are outside this code construction.

**Experiment/falsifier.** Enumerate multiplicity vectors for \(8\le N\le16\); derive \(\mathcal O,\mathfrak P\), and \(34\) explicitly; optimize all false fibers. Most likely death: redundancy raises legal and adverse energies proportionally.

2. **Affine-polymorphism universal refuter**

**Core trick.** View every equality-only gate library as an integral affine relation and compute its closure under the Maltsev operation \(p(x,y,z)=x-y+z\). Search for an idempotent grade-zero polymorphism that recursively converts legal local sections into a false-root zero-defect section with bounded negative-coordinate density.

**Expected move.** Refute the FRONTIER for every library satisfying a checkable closure hypothesis, rather than finding another isolated attack. A depth-independent density bound would contradict the required \((17/16)^h\) adverse growth.

**Obstruction audit.** This deliberately generalizes G2 affine/Graver, G3 unbounded fibers, G5 private overlap, G9/G11 parity, G13 affine collision, G15 laminar lift, G19 signed flow, GD1 diagonal closure, and the ordered-pair splice; it is inside their assumptions as an attack. G1 RS slack, G6 quotient, G7 radix, and G12/DROP are included by requiring all emitted rows and the zero vector. G14, G31, and G38 are finite evidence only. G28 min-plus, G32 additive parity, and G37 parity cut motivate the recursive closure test, but no transfer table is inferred. G30 tensor, GD2/A5 convolution, G33/G34 tags, D4 shells, and E6 ports are unused.

**Experiment/falsifier.** For every \(N\le12\) redundant module, compute saturated affine polymorphisms over \(\mathbb Z\) and \(\mathbb F_{289}\), then synthesize depth-three recursive attacks. The mechanism dies if boundary forcing destroys every composable polymorphism or negative density grows exponentially.

G28, G32, and G37 are avoided because the proof is filtration-exact, not min-plus or additive composition. **Experiment/falsifier.** For one redundant NAND and COPY candidate, compute graded kernels/cokernels over \(\mathbb F_{289}\), solve exactly for a contracting homotopy, then verify depth two over \(\mathcal O/\mathfrak P^2\). Most likely death: a semantic affine class survives as adverse \(H_0\).

**Obstruction audit.** G1/G6/G7/G12 are avoided by emitting normalization, cylinders, carries, and DROP. G2/G3 demand unrestricted signed-measure optimization and remain a proof obligation. 7. **Parametric Graver-circuit attack**

**Core trick.** Build exact depth-one through depth-three adverse matrices and search for a recursively stable Graver circuit \(g_{h+1}=E(g_h)+c\) with valuation zero. Once discovered, prove the recurrence symbolically from the module’s block matrix, yielding an all-depth counterexample rather than extrapolating a finite shell.

**Expected move.** Refute a proposed gate library—or potentially the FRONTIER for a structural class—by exhibiting zero-residual energy growth no faster than the legal baseline.

**Obstruction audit.** This intentionally weaponizes G2 affine/Graver and G3 unbounded fibers, with all G1 slack, G6 quotient, G7 radix, and G12/DROP rows present. Seeds must include G5 private overlap, G9/G11 parity, G13 collision, G15 lift, G19 flow, GD1 closure, and ordered-pair splice. G14/G31/G38 remain mere seed sources. Unlike the prohibited inference in G28 min-plus, G32 additive parity, or G37 parity cut, promotion occurs only after verifying an exact symbolic circuit recurrence. No G30 tensor is assumed. GD2/A5 bicyclic patterns are included as sparse circuit seeds, but no convolution algebra is required. G33/G34, D4, and E6 assumptions are irrelevant.

**Experiment/falsifier.** Generate depth \(1,2,3\) matrices for each surviving redundant module; compute short conformal kernel circuits and solve for a block-recursive embedding. The attack dies if supports necessarily expand faster than legal rank or acquire positive \(\mathfrak P\)-valuation at every lift.
