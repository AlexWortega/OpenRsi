# Ramsey attack log

## Imported baseline

Read all of `prior/` and reran `prior/verify_ramsey.py` successfully. The imported results are PARTIAL: explicit `K_16`/`K_32` seeds, fixed-layer nonextension, exact composition and capacity identities, local-palette reductions, and obstructions to iid product methods. No superexponential construction exists in the imported work. Precise gap: correlated strong-power codes/colorings with growing per-color base.

## Three code-driven routes on the gap

1. **Resolve unrestricted `F_2^6` four-color partition.** Encode one-hot colors on 63 nonzero vectors and forbid monochromatic projective lines. First experiment: `experiments/sat_f2_6_4color.py` using PySAT/CaDiCaL with color symmetry breaking. SAT gives an explicit `K_64` four-color translation-invariant seed; UNSAT gives a checkable solver result but will require independently reproducible proof logging before being claimed rigorous.
2. **Correlated strong-power code search.** Choose high-fractional-chromatic triangle-free complements (small Mycielski/Cayley graphs), encode an independent set in power 3 or 4 by SAT/maximum clique, and compare achieved base against `sqrt(5)`. First experiment planned: exact cardinality SAT for Grötzsch power 3, with symmetry-fixed initial codewords and explicit output verification.
3. **Cayley/local-palette algebraic search.** Search color rules on growing finite groups where color classes are sum-free but colors are reused through cosets or shifted layers rather than a single difference partition. First experiment planned: SAT over two-layer `F_2^d` rules allowing layer-dependent permutations and extra cross-layer correlations; test whether the rigid nonextension obstruction disappears while global/local palette accounting improves.

## Immediate runs and harvested results

Installed PySAT in local `.venv` and launched the unrestricted `F_2^6` SAT instance. This is 252 Boolean variables before fixed literals and 651 projective-line constraints times four colors, plus one-hot clauses. Added sound stronger symmetry breaking: choose a largest color class (size at least 16); it has rank at least five because a 4-dimensional subspace has only 15 nonzero vectors. A linear automorphism can therefore send five independent members of that class to vectors `1,2,4,8,16`, so fixing those five to color zero loses no solutions. Branching the sixth basis vector into color 0 or a representative nonzero color is exhaustive: permutations of colors 1,2,3 remain after fixing color 0. Both branches were later terminated without answers; no SAT/UNSAT conclusion is claimed.

The first exact correlated-code SAT nudge succeeded. For the 11-vertex Grötzsch triangle-free complement, a 12-word strong-cube code was found and independently checked by `verify_grotzsch_code.py`. Thus the associated independence-two graph has capacity at least `12^(1/3)>sqrt(5)`, improving the prior finite `C_5` benchmark. This remains only a fixed constant base. The target-13 SAT instance and greedy scans on 23/47-vertex iterated Mycielski graphs did not finish/improve after the allotted nudges; they were terminated and this fixed-Mycielski route is banked.

Switched to the algebraic family. Min-conflicts found explicit sum-free partitions of `F_2^7 minus {0}` into 5 classes and `F_2^8 minus {0}` into 6 classes, yielding checked `K_128`/5-color and `K_256`/6-color translation colorings. `verify_f2_partitions.py` checks every class and all induced triangles. Their bases are only `2^(7/5)` and `2^(8/6)`, hence fixed and below the prompt's cited classical base; they are structural data, not asymptotic progress. Searches for the more consequential ratios `(d,k)=(8,5)` and `(9,6)` stalled far from zero (best 34 and 33 conflicts) and were terminated after the second nudge. The `F_2^6` 4-color exact SAT and local search likewise remained unresolved at one conflict; no claim.

A correlated block-composition nudge encoded colors as functions of the two seed color/zero states rather than first nonzero block. It found a 10-color rule on `F_2^14`, independently checked directly over all 44,731,051 projective lines by `verify_block_composition.py`; inspection shows it is numerically just the ordinary sum of two five-color palettes. Eight-output SAT returned UNSAT but without a proof certificate, so this is not claimed as a finite theorem; nine-output searches stalled.

**Adversarial correction:** the later claimed 13-color three-block compression was invalid. Both its search and verifier discarded a projective line whenever two coarse state vectors agreed (`len(set(ss))<3`), but distinct underlying vectors can have identical coarse states. The archived mapping colors the explicit distinct line `16384, 196608, 212992` monochromatically. `verify_retracted_block_power_3_13.py` now verifies that explicit monochromatic witness and exits 0. The mapping and all recursively derived mappings were moved to `experiments/invalid/`; their claims are retracted. The corrected search includes 2-state constraints. A corrected 14-color min-conflicts run reached one violated constraint, then stalled for more than nine CPU-minutes and was terminated; a second implementation initialized from a proper coloring of the repeated-state graph again reached one conflict and timed out. An exact SAT encoding and two sound symmetry branches of the unresolved `F_2^6` four-color partition each produced no answer after 9–12 CPU-minutes and were terminated. These are heuristic/time-limit non-results, not UNSAT claims. `verify_block_power_constraints.py` independently regenerates the banked hypergraph: 215 states, 12,730 two-state constraints, and 410,455 three-state constraints. After two nudges this coarse-state compression route is stalled.

The next code-driven route returned to correlated strong-power codes at larger family scale. Completed greedy cube-code searches on the 47- and 95-vertex iterated Mycielski triangle-free graphs found only size 12, exactly the existing Grötzsch benchmark. Together with the earlier 23/47-vertex nudges this family is banked as stalled; no upper bound or impossibility is claimed.

A different algebraic route searched translation colorings on cyclic groups. A preliminary symmetric-sum-free-set/dilate-cover construction gave only fixed bases near two and deteriorated with group size. Direct min-conflicts partitioning was more effective: it found explicit symmetric sum-free partitions for `(p,k)=(127,5),(251,6),(509,7),(1021,8)`. `verify_cyclic_colorings.py` checks every partition, symmetry, sum-freeness, and all vertex triangles (up to 176,867,830 triangles for `p=1021`). The per-color bases decrease from about 2.634 to 2.375, so this coherent-looking roughly-doubling sequence remains fixed-base and below the prompt's classical benchmark. The final nudge also succeeded: `Z_2039 minus {0}` partitions into 9 symmetric sum-free classes, and the verifier checks all 1,410,786,139 triangles. The base is about `2039^(1/9)=2.332`, continuing the decline. This coherent cyclic family is therefore banked: it is strong finite evidence for an easy roughly-doubling pattern, but exactly the wrong asymptotic scale for the target.

A new Cayley-capacity experiment searched the *strong cubes* of complements defined by random symmetric sum-free cyclic sets on 17 and 31 vertices. After roughly six CPU-minutes each, both found only size 11, below the verified Grötzsch size 12. Together with the cyclic partition family this is a second negative nudge for this Cayley family, so it is banked as stalled; no upper bound is claimed.

The next route attacked the local-palette seed criterion directly rather than through translation invariance. The first palette design (balanced 4-subsets of six colors) remained hundreds of conflicts away on `K_40,K_50` and was terminated. Reducing to five global colors changed the behavior sharply: explicit locally-4 triangle-free colorings of `K_20`, `K_25`, and `K_26` were found. `verify_local_palette_seed.py` independently checks every edge/palette incidence and every triangle. Their best iteration base is only `26^(1/4)≈2.258`, so they are structurally new but numerically weak. Searches at orders 27, 30, 35, and 40 all stalled (respectively at 1, 12, 30, and 82 conflicts) after several CPU-minutes and were terminated. The same palette design succeeded through 26 but gives no scalable pattern; after two size nudges this local-palette instantiation was banked. A one-step larger analogue (`g=6,s=5`) then found verified seeds on 50, 55, 56, and 57 vertices. `verify_local_palette_seed.py` checks all incidences and triangles. Its best iteration base `57^(1/5)≈2.245` is still weak. Searches at 58–75 stalled (best conflicts 9,17,4,32,112) and were terminated after the second size nudge. This ad hoc local-palette hierarchy is banked as fixed-base finite structure; analysis of missing-color groups showed no clean quotient/blow-up rule.

A more structured variant asks for large induced subsets of a verified translation coloring where every selected vertex misses a color. An exact SAT formulation on the cyclic seeds found a 40-vertex locally-4 induced subcoloring of `K_127`/5 and a 60-vertex locally-5 induced subcoloring of `K_251`/6, independently checked by `verify_cyclic_local_subset.py`. Continuing through the cyclic `K_509`/7 and `K_1021`/8 seeds gives verified locally-6 `K_100` and locally-7 `K_160`. The four bases decrease `2.515,2.268,2.154,2.065`, so the structured induced-subset family is decisively at fixed-base scale and is banked after two extensions. Harder targets 45/50 on the first seed and 80 on the second remained running without answers; they were terminated once the larger-seed trend became clear. Joint optimization of palettes and edges was also tried on `(N,g,s)=(30,8,4),(40,8,4)`, but a crude penalty objective remained hundreds of violations away and was terminated rather than tuned further.

A new interval-difference route searches colors of positive integers so no `a,b,b-a` are monochromatic, i.e. translation colorings of `[N]`. Min-conflicts runs at `(N,k)=(256,6),(512,7),(1024,8)` stalled for nine CPU-minutes, but an exact SAT encoding solved them immediately and also found `(128,5),(2048,9)`. `verify_interval_difference.py` independently checks all `a,b,b-a` constraints. The resulting sequence is exactly another doubling family `N=2^(k+2)`, with base tending to two, so it is banked after the exact nudge. The lesson is methodological: the heuristic stall was not evidence of nonexistence.

Returned once to missing-color structure with a stronger requirement: every selected vertex misses at least two colors. The first SAT formulation falsely returned UNSAT even for three vertices because it imposed missing-color cardinality on unselected vertices; adversarial sanity checks at `N=3` caught this. After replacing it by conditional cardinality clauses, verified cyclic induced subsets have `(N,g,missing)=(10,5,2),(15,6,2),(25,7,2)`. `verify_multi_missing_subsets.py` checks all triangles and missing palettes. Larger two-missing targets on the first two seeds stalled for nine CPU-minutes and were terminated, while the `g=7,8` seeds yielded verified `K_50,K_80`. A three-missing extension yielded verified `(N,g,t)=(15,7,3),(25,8,3)`; `verify_three_missing_subsets.py` passes, and a `g=9,N=40` target was attempted. However the relevant bases `N^(1/(g-t))` remain between 1.90 and 2.19. Thus even increasing the number of missing colors has not changed the fixed-base scale. The final `(40,9,3)` target produced no answer after nine CPU-minutes and was terminated; this multi-missing cyclic route is banked.

A finite-field slope-coloring idea was rejected immediately and rigorously at the model level: three collinear points have all three edges in one direction, creating a one-state monochromatic obstruction for any color rule depending only on slope. No search result is claimed. Target 70 on `K_127` was SAT-solver UNSAT, but no proof certificate is retained, so no impossibility is claimed.

A ternary vector-space translation search briefly appeared to find an `F_3^4`/5 partition, but adversarial independent verification caught a fatal modeling error: the search omitted equal summands. In exponent three, symmetry forces `x` and `-x=2x` to share a color, so the triple `(x,x,2x)` immediately violates sum-freeness. The output was archived as invalid, all dependent searches were killed, and the generator now detects this one-state obstruction. No ternary construction is claimed.

A shifted cyclic experiment then allowed colors on `Z_p times [r]` to depend on both the layer pair and oriented difference, breaking full translation-product rigidity. Independent direct verification confirms shifted seeds `(N,k)=(62,5),(93,6),(254,6),(381,7)`, checking up to 9,145,270 triangles. The best base is `254^(1/6)≈2.517`, still below existing finite seeds. These show layers can be added with one extra color in the tested cases, but that still gives only fixed-base behavior. Directly scaling `p` to 509 was too expensive during constraint generation and was terminated. A more discriminating nudge fixed `p` and asked whether five layers can be absorbed with no extra colors (`31x5/5`, `127x5/6`). These stalled at 18 and 267 conflicts after nearly three CPU-minutes and were terminated. The shifted-layer route is banked after two nudges.

Switched to nonabelian group-difference colorings. Search found inverse-closed product-free partitions of dihedral groups of orders `32,64,128,256,512,1024` into `4,5,6,7,8,9` classes. `verify_dihedral_colorings.py` checks inverse closure, all within-class products, and all vertex triangles. This is a coherent nonabelian doubling family but still fixed-base; The pattern persists exactly through order 1024. Its base is `2^(10/9)≈2.16` at the last seed and declines to 2, so the family is now banked as another coherent but asymptotically useless doubling construction. Symmetric groups were immediately blocked by a one-state obstruction from odd-order elements, correctly detected rather than misclaimed. This materially restores the prior conclusion: no verified correlated compression beyond disjoint palettes has been found.
