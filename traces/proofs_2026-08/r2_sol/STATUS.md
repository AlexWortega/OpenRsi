A 3,000-sample directional stress test completed: the sharp simplex has best tested certificate score `4>8/3`, while random high-volume samples remained far below the target volume and mostly also failed the certificate. This is heuristic diagnosis, not a finite theorem, and supplies no new inequality. A final pyramid reduction identifies the still-unproved planar inequality `area(uB cap -vB)/area(B)>=(3-z^2)/8`; it would imply the partial bound `vol(P)<=16` for centered lattice-free 3D pyramids. Only the triangular equality formula is verified. The unrestricted three-dimensional gap remains open.
