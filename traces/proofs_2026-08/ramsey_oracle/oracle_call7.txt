## Candidate: balanced generalized-quadrangle Tanner codes

This is one concrete, falsifiable candidate. The pivotal rigidity lemma below is unproved; everything else is explicit.

### 1. Objects and coloring rule

#### The triangle-free coordinate graph

Let  
\[
q=2^{2s+1}\qquad (s\ge 0),
\]
and let \(\mathcal Q_q=W(q)\) be the classical symplectic generalized quadrangle of order \((q,q)\), equipped with its standard involutory Tits polarity
\[
\pi_q:P_q\longleftrightarrow L_q.
\]
Its incidence table is directly computable over \(\mathbb F_q\); \(\pi_q\) uses the field automorphism \(x\mapsto x^{2^{s+1}}\). We have
\[
|P_q|=|L_q|=n_q=(q+1)(q^2+1).
\]

Define a simple graph \(H_q\) on \(P_q\) by
\[
x\sim_{H_q}y
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
x\ne y\ \text{ and }\ x\mathrel I\pi_q(y).
\]

**Exact triangle-free proof.** If distinct \(x,y,z\) formed a triangle, then the incidence graph of \(\mathcal Q_q\) would contain the six-cycle
\[
x-\pi_q(y)-z-\pi_q(x)-y-\pi_q(z)-x.
\]
The three point vertices and three line vertices are distinct because \(\pi_q\) is bijective. Generalized-quadrangle incidence graphs have girth eight, contradiction.

#### The Tanner graphs

Start with the Tutte–Coxeter graph \(T_2\), viewed as a cubic bipartite graph with \(15\) vertices on each side and \(45\) edges.

For \(q_s=2^{2s+1}\), define \(T_{q_s}\) recursively by taking six successive \(2\)-lifts from \(T_{q_{s-1}}\). At each lift, order all edge signings lexicographically and choose the first signing whose new eigenvalues have absolute value at most \(2\sqrt2\). Existence follows from the standard interlacing-polynomial theorem for bipartite Ramanujan \(2\)-lifts. This is a deterministic finite algorithm, independent of any Ramsey search.

Write
\[
T_q=(A_q,B_q,E_q).
\]
Then
\[
|A_q|=|B_q|=15(q/2)^3,\qquad
K_q:=|E_q|=\frac{45}{8}q^3.
\]

#### Configurations

A configuration is a labeling
\[
f:E_q\to P_q
\]
satisfying:

1. **Collinear-star constraint:** at every \(v\in A_q\cup B_q\), the three labels on the incident edges are distinct and lie on one line of \(\mathcal Q_q\).

2. **Equitable type constraint:** for all \(p,p'\in P_q\),
   \[
   \bigl||f^{-1}(p)|-|f^{-1}(p')|\bigr|\le 1.
   \]

Let \(\mathscr X_q\) be the set of all such configurations.

Let
\[
G_q=\operatorname{Aut}(\mathcal Q_q,\pi_q)
\]
act by postcomposition. Define \(C_q\subseteq\mathscr X_q\) to contain the lexicographically least configuration from each \(G_q\)-orbit. This quotient removes global geometric symmetries and costs only \(q^{O(1)}\), since \(|G_q|=q^{O(1)}\).

Regard every \(f\in C_q\) as a word in \(P_q^{E_q}\).

For distinct \(f,g\in C_q\), choose the least edge \(e\in E_q\) such that
\[
f(e)\sim_{H_q}g(e),
\]
and color \(\{f,g\}\) by \(e\).

Because \(H_q\) is triangle-free, three configurations cannot have all three pairwise edges colored by the same \(e\). Thus this is a triangle-free \(K_q\)-coloring whenever every pair has such a coordinate.

### 2. Required asymptotics

At one cubic check, the number of ordered legal triples is exactly
\[
n_q(q+1)q(q-1)=q^{6+o(1)},
\]
whereas the total number of ordered triples is \(n_q^3=q^{9+o(1)}\). Thus one check has density \(q^{-3+o(1)}\).

There are \(2K_q/3\) checks and \(n_q^{K_q}=q^{(3+o(1))K_q}\) unrestricted edge-labelings. The Bethe/first-moment prediction is therefore
\[
|\mathscr X_q|
=
q^{(3+o(1))K_q}
\left(q^{-3+o(1)}\right)^{2K_q/3}
=
q^{(1+o(1))K_q}.
\]
Conditioning on equitable type costs only \(\exp(O(K_q))=q^{o(K_q)}\), since \(K_q=\Theta(n_q)\).

Hence any bound
\[
|C_q|\ge q^{cK_q}
\]
with absolute \(c>0\) gives
\[
|C_q|
\ge K_q^{(c/3+o(1))K_q},
\]
because \(K_q=\Theta(q^3)\). This is exactly the desired growing base.

### 3. Pivotal lemma and proof program

#### Balanced GQ–Tanner rigidity lemma

There is an absolute \(c>0\) such that, for every \(q=2^{2s+1}\):

1. \[
   |\mathscr X_q/G_q|\ge q^{cK_q};
   \]

2. if \(f,g\in\mathscr X_q\) satisfy
   \[
   f(e)\not\sim_{H_q}g(e)
   \qquad\text{for every }e\in E_q,
   \]
   then
   \[
   g=\sigma\circ f
   \quad\text{for some }\sigma\in G_q.
   \]

Part 2 implies that distinct canonical orbit representatives are separated in some coordinate.

This is not an arbitrary-capacity restatement: \(\mathscr X_q\) is the full solution set of one fixed arity-three incidence constraint on one fixed explicit expander tower. No codeword subset is selected by greedy separation.

#### Specific proof strategy

**Entropy part.**

Let
\[
S(p_1,p_2,p_3)=1
\]
when \(p_1,p_2,p_3\) are distinct collinear points. All one- and two-coordinate marginals of \(S\) are explicit and uniform. Flattenings of \(S\) reduce to the point-line incidence matrix of \(W(q)\), whose singular values are explicitly known.

The intended proof is:

1. Compute the exact second moment of the partition function on a random \(2\)-lift, organized by joint point types.
2. Use the incidence spectrum to show that the uniform joint type is the unique maximizer, with every nonuniform type losing \(q^{\Omega(K_q)}\).
3. Iterate this estimate along the six-lifts-per-level tower, using the selected Ramanujan signing to control the nontrivial Fourier modes.
4. Extract equitable configurations by taking the corresponding multivariate coefficient. Since each point occurs \(O(1)\) times on average, Stirling’s formula loses only \(\exp(O(K_q))\), negligible on the \(K_q\log q\) scale.

**Rigidity part.**

For a purported bad pair \(f,g\), every Tanner check gives two ordered collinear triples
\[
(f(e_1),f(e_2),f(e_3)),
\qquad
(g(e_1),g(e_2),g(e_3)),
\]
with all three diagonal pairs nonadjacent in the polarity graph.

The proof should proceed through the following finite-geometry classification.

1. **Local phase classification.** Classify, modulo \(G_q\), pairs of ordered collinear triples with the three forbidden diagonal incidences. Using the generalized-quadrangle unique-projection axiom and absence of six-cycles, show that every such pair determines a partial polarity-preserving collineation on the union of their radius-two incidence neighborhoods. The ambiguity is the stabilizer of the first ordered triple.

2. **Transition consistency.** If two Tanner checks share edge \(e\), their local phases agree on \(f(e)\mapsto g(e)\). Classify incompatible transitions explicitly. Every incompatible transition forces all labels in a fixed positive-radius Tanner neighborhood into one proper geometric set: a line pencil, a perp set, or a proper subquadrangle.

3. **Defect amplification.** Ramanujan expansion implies that a nonempty set of incompatible transitions expands to a positive proportion of checks. The incidence spectrum of \(W(q)\) then implies that a positive proportion of all edge labels lie in one of the proper geometric sets above.

4. **Use equitability.** Each such proper set contains \(O(q^2)\) of the \(n_q=\Theta(q^3)\) points. Equitable type permits only \(O(1/q)\) of all labels there, contradicting the positive proportion from Step 3.

5. Therefore all local phases synchronize to one global \(\sigma\in G_q\), and connectedness gives \(g=\sigma\circ f\).

The decisive work is the local phase table and the quantitative defect-amplification inequality. Both depend on the rank-two incidence geometry and are not generic graph-capacity assertions.

### 4. Smallest discriminating test

Use \(q=2\).

- \(\mathcal Q_2\) is the doily \(GQ(2,2)\), with \(15\) points and \(15\) lines.
- \(T_2\) is the Tutte–Coxeter graph, with \(45\) edges.
- Every check constraint says that its three labels are exactly the three points of one doily line.
- Equitability says that every point occurs exactly three times.

Construct a SAT instance with variables
\[
f_e,g_e\in P_2,\qquad e\in E(T_2),
\]
and constraints:

1. each incident triple of \(f\), and separately of \(g\), is one of the \(15\cdot 3!=90\) ordered line triples;
2. every point appears exactly three times in each configuration;
3. for every \(e\),
   \[
   f_e\not\sim_{H_2}g_e;
   \]
4. for every \(\sigma\in G_2\),
   \[
   g\ne \sigma\circ f.
   \]

A satisfying assignment immediately falsifies the rigidity mechanism as stated. The instance has only \(90\) point-valued variables; \(G_2\) can be enumerated from the incidence table. Before full SAT, enumerate the \(90^2\) pairs of legal local triples and their transition orbits—failure of the proposed local-phase classification already kills the proof strategy.

This route is neither a fixed seed nor an iid word construction: \(q\), the coordinate graph, the Tanner graph, and the achieved predicted base all grow, while the expander constraints correlate all coordinates.
